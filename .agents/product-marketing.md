# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-02*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:**  
ADealForge is a B2B lead generation platform in development that currently delivers managed outbound email campaigns as a service.

**What it does:**  
We run an end-to-end outbound workflow for clients: define target market criteria, build company lists in any niche, research each company online, generate personalized first-contact messaging, and automate email outreach.  
The internal process and tooling are already operational and used in real campaigns.

**Product category:**  
B2B lead generation and outbound sales automation.

**Product type:**  
SaaS platform (in progress) + managed service execution (live now).

**Business model:**  
Current: manual, client-specific outbound delivery while the platform is being built.  
Future: self-serve or semi-managed SaaS.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:**  
B2B companies that need a predictable pipeline of potential clients through outbound email.

**Decision-makers:**  
Founders, growth leads, heads of sales, and business development leaders.

**Primary use case:**  
Launch or scale outbound lead generation without building an in-house prospecting and personalization operation.

**Jobs to be done:**  
- Find ICP-fit companies in a chosen market.
- Turn raw company data into qualified outreach opportunities.
- Send personalized first-touch email campaigns at scale.

**Use cases:**  
- Early-stage teams that need pipeline fast.
- Teams testing new verticals or geographies.
- Companies that want a "done-for-you" outbound engine before adopting software.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:**  
Most teams cannot consistently produce high-quality, personalized outbound at scale.

**Why alternatives fall short:**  
- Generic lead lists are low quality.
- Manual research is too slow.
- Personalization is difficult to scale without a workflow.

**What it costs them:**  
Lost pipeline, low reply rates, wasted team time, and delayed revenue opportunities.

**Emotional tension:**  
Uncertainty about pipeline growth and frustration from inconsistent outbound results.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**  
- End-to-end workflow from prospect discovery to outreach execution.
- Cross-vertical targeting capabilities (can work with almost any niche).
- Human-verified, context-aware first-contact messaging.
- Proven ability to operate at high volume (campaigns reaching 100,000 companies).

**How we do it differently:**  
We combine repeatable internal tooling with managed execution, so clients get results now instead of waiting for full product rollout.

**Why that's better:**  
Clients can start outbound quickly with a reliable process, while benefiting from the systems that will power the future SaaS.

**Why customers choose us:**  
They need practical execution, not just software access.

## Proof Points
**Metrics:**  
- Campaign operations scaled to outreach for up to 100,000 companies.

**Value themes:**  
- Speed to launch outbound campaigns.
- Better relevance in first-touch messages.
- Structured, repeatable lead generation operations.
